🪪 NIDA Tanzania — How to Apply

  1. Visit your nearest NIDA registration center (district or regional office) — locations are listed on the official NIDA website.
  2. Bring an original ID document (birth certificate, voter's ID, or passport) plus a photocopy.
  3. Complete the biometric registration (fingerprints + photo) on-site — there's no need to bring your own photo.
  4. You'll receive a registration/application number — keep it safe, it's used to track your application status.
  5. Processing can take from a few weeks to several months depending on center backlog — check status via NIDA's official channels using your application number.
⚠️ This is general public information, not official NIDA guidance. Requirements and offices change — always verify with nida.go.tz or your local registration center directly.

💰 Payment APIs in Tanzania — Overview

  • ClickPesa — USSD Push for M-Pesa/Tigo Pesa/Airtel, webhook callbacks, HMAC-SHA256 request signing.
  • Selcom — payment gateway supporting mobile money + cards, popular for e-commerce.
  • DPO Group (Direct Pay Online) — regional gateway covering multiple East African payment methods.
  • Mobile Network Operator APIs — direct integrations with M-Pesa (Vodacom), Tigo Pesa, and Airtel Money, each with their own developer portals and approval process.
